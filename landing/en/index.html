Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer here? Write to us.

What is AI visibility?

How often, and in what position, your business is mentioned in the answers that assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity give to customer questions.

We already do SEO. Isn’t that enough?

SEO affects your search rankings. AI answers look at different signals. A site that ranks well on Google may never appear in AI answers.

How is the score calculated?

Questions relevant to your industry are asked to several models; we measure whether you’re mentioned, in what position and in what context.

Who carries out the action plan?

You, your team or your agency. Every item is written so it can be understood without technical knowledge.

When will I see results?

We measure each action’s impact after 21 days. Models update at different speeds, so we don’t promise a fixed timeline.
